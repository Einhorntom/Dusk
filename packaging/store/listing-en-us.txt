=== Description ===

Control your monitors from the Windows tray: brightness, contrast, volume, input source and color, without reaching for the buttons on the back of the screen.

Save your favorite setups as presets, such as a bright "Day" and a warm, dim "Night", and switch between them with one click, a global hotkey or a command. Dusk also adjusts your laptop's built-in display, so one hotkey can dim every screen at once.

Dusk talks to external monitors over DDC/CI, the standard control channel built into HDMI, DisplayPort and USB-C, and shows only the controls each monitor actually supports.

Gentle with your hardware:
• Changes are sent once you stop adjusting, unchanged values are never rewritten, and writes are rate-limited to spare the monitor's memory.
• Switching input asks first and switches back automatically unless you keep it, which matters when the monitor is also your USB hub.
• A monitor that stops responding times out instead of freezing the app.

Light and private: one small tray app with no network access, no telemetry and no account. Your settings live in a single file you can read and edit.

For power users: the included dusk command (JSON output and exit codes for scripts), and PowerToys Run and Command Palette integrations, available from the project page on GitHub.

Requirements: Windows 11 (x64). External monitors need DDC/CI turned on, usually an option in the monitor's on-screen menu. Tested on a Lenovo L32p-30 over USB-C and a laptop's built-in display; other monitors work through capability discovery.

Free and open source (MIT license): https://github.com/Einhorntom/Dusk
